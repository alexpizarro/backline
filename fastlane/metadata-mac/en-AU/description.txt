Backline is a practice player for musicians, dancers and singers. Pick a song, split it into parts on your Mac, and practise.

Everything happens on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, there's no account, and Backline doesn't track you.

THREE WAYS TO PRACTISE
• Play — mute or solo any part, even just the lead guitar, and play along with the band.
• Dance — built for bachata and salsa. Hear the güira or any one instrument on its own, tap on the 1, and Backline counts to 8 for you.
• Beats — the vocals come out and the drums split into kick, snare and hi-hats, so you can rap or sing over the beat.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Mute or solo vocals, drums, bass, guitars, keys and other instruments.
• Split the lead and rhythm guitar apart.
• Split the drum kit into kick, snare, hi-hats and other drums (an optional one-time download).
• Slow a song down or speed it up without losing pitch.
• Shift the pitch by frets.
• Loop any section and add a count-in.
• Save the result as a backing track.

GETTING SONGS IN
Drop in an audio or video file, record a song while it plays in another app, record a band with a microphone or an audio interface, or paste a link to a song or video. Backline keeps everything on your Mac.

Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account, no tracking. Open source. Requires macOS 15.0 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.
