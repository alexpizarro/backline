https://github.com/alexpizarro/backline/releases/tag/models-1 and checks each file's SHA-256.

BS-RoFormer SW (6-part separation, the "cleaner parts" pass) - checkpoint "BS-Roformer SW" trained by
jarredou and shared with the music source separation community. No licence was published with the
checkpoint. Architecture: BS-RoFormer (Lu et al., "Music Source Separation with Band-Split RoPE
Transformer", ByteDance, 2023). Reference code: ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training (MIT).
Converted to Core ML (16-bit) for Backline. Mirror: https://huggingface.co/FractalSurfer/BS-RoFormer-SW-6stem

MDX23C DrumSep (drum kit split: kick, snare, toms, hi-hat, cymbals) - checkpoint by jarredou, building on
the DrumSep work of aufr33 and jarredou. Creative Commons BY-NC-SA 4.0. Architecture: TFC-TDF-v3 (MDX23C,
Kim et al.). Reference code: ZFTurbo/Music-Source-Separation-Training (MIT). Converted to Core ML (16-bit)
for Backline, a free, non-commercial app. Mirror: https://huggingface.co/dshdaaf/separation-models
